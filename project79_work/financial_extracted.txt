[P6] KEY FACTORS AFFECTING OUR RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
[P7] Our results of operations, financial condition and the period-to-period comparability of our financial results are principally affected by the following factors:
[P8] Development of the AI Industry and Demand for AI Infrastructure
[P9] The development of the AI industry and the increasing demand for AI model training, inference, agentic applications and enterprise AI adoption have been key drivers of our financial performance. As AI models increase in scale and complexity, customers require more efficient coordination of heterogeneous computing resources, stable training and inference operations, better observability and more cost-efficient token generation. These market requirements have supported demand for our agentic platform software, AGI enterprise solutions and Agentic MaaS.
[P10] Commercialization of Our Products and Services
[P11] Our financial performance depends on the pace and scale at which we commercialize our products and services. During the Track Record Period, we generated revenue from agentic platform software, AGI enterprise solutions and Agentic MaaS. Our agentic platform software can be deployed through private on-premises environments and public cloud infrastructure and offered under license arrangements or subscription-based pricing models. Our AGI enterprise solutions deliver deployable AI solutions for vertical enterprise scenarios. Our Agentic MaaS is delivered through managed model-serving infrastructure and usage-based service arrangements. 
[P12] Product Capabilities and Technological Strength
[P13] Our ability to meet customer requirements and to scale revenue depends on our product capabilities and technological strength. Our agentic platform abstracts the differences among heterogeneous computing resources, provides unified orchestration and intelligent operations management, supports model-aware workload adaptation and optimization, and provides dynamic workload scheduling, elastic resource allocation, operational reliability and observability. These capabilities support large-scale AI model training and inference workloads and help customers improve computing efficiency, deployment stability and operational automation.
[P14] Access to Computing Resources and Cost Management
[P15] Our ability to manage cost of sales is important to our gross profit margin. Our products and services require access to substantial AI computing capacity to support model training, inference and MaaS operations. We procure computing resources from third-party providers, including data center operators, AI computing cluster providers and cloud or infrastructure service providers, and integrate such resources with our proprietary platform, optimization technologies and operational capabilities. Our gross profit margin may be affected by our ability to procure computing resources and other service inputs efficiently, improve resource utilization, standardize delivery processes and optimize technical support and implementation costs.
[P16] Continued Investment in R&D and Talent
[P17] Our ability to maintain technological competitiveness depends on continued investment in R&D and talent. Our R&D expenses amounted to RMB30.6 million, RMB252.9 million, RMB354.7 million, RMB[●] million and RMB93.4 million in 2023, 2024, 2025 and for the three months ended March 31, 2025 and 2026, respectively. During the Track Record Period, our R&D expenses increased significantly as we expanded our R&D team and continued to develop heterogeneous computing technologies, inference optimization technologies, model-serving capabilities and agentic infrastructure. Such investment supports our product capabilities and commercialization but materially affects our profitability in the short to medium term.

[P22] NON-IFRS MEASURE
[P23] To supplement our consolidated financial statements, we use adjusted net profit/(loss) (non-IFRS measure) as additional financial measure, which is not required by, or presented in accordance with IFRS Accounting Standards. We define adjusted net profit/(loss) (non-IFRS measure) as profit/(loss) for the period/year adjusted by adding back changes in fair values of financial liabilities on shares with preferential rights, listing expenses and share-based payment expenses. Changes in fair values of financial liabilities on shares with preferential rights reflect changes in our valuation and equity financing activities, which will be automatically converted into ordinary shares upon the Listing and the related financial instruments will be reclassified from financial liabilities to equity. Listing expenses are the expenses arising from activities in relation to the Listing and Global Offering. Share-based payment expenses are non-cash in nature and arise from granting economic rights in our share incentive platforms to senior management and employees. ur presentation of adjusted net profit/(loss) (non-IFRS measure) may not be comparable to similarly titled measures presented by other companies. The use of this non-IFRS measure as an analytical tool has limitations, and you should not consider them in isolation from, or as a substitute for an analysis of, our results of operations or financial conditions as reported under IFRS Accounting Standards.
